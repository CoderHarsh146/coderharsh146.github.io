PROMPT EXAMPLE

Is example ka prompt storyboard ke action, framing aur matched video movement ko describe karta hai.

VIDEO GENERATION PROMPT16:9 · 8 SEC
Create an 8-second cinematic 16:9 tracking shot of young Shri Krishna and his gurukul companion walking together along a forest path after collecting firewood. Keep their approved character appearance, facial features, hair, clothing, body proportions and relative height consistent with the attached character references and storyboard. The camera follows them from behind at a steady, slow pace as they move deeper through a lush green forest. Soft golden sunlight filters through the trees; leaves and the edges of their clothing move gently in a natural breeze. Keep both characters visible in the frame and preserve the storyboard's action and composition. Use smooth, physically plausible movement, a restrained camera and a cinematic 3D animated feature-film look. No sudden camera movement, no scene cuts, no extra characters, no on-screen text, no captions, no logos and no watermark.
Prompt camera, framing, action aur movement ko specify karta hai. Generated output tool ke hisaab se vary kar sakta hai.
