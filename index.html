07 / TWO PRODUCTION APPROACHES

Two approaches.
One cinematic goal.

APPROACH / 01

Storyboard → Video Clips

Used for · Shri Krishna–Sudama Milan

Plan a scene in sequential frames, approve the visual blueprint, then generate video clips guided by the storyboard and locked references.

  1. Script & voiceover planningDevelop the full Hindi story and prepare character-specific voiceover scripts.
  2. Character and location referencesGenerate and save character sheets; add location/object references when useful for the story.
  3. Storyboard prompt & generationCreate a sequential 3×3 storyboard sheet using a strict visual format.
  4. Review → revise → approveInspect story flow and visual continuity. Approval acts as the checkpoint before video generation.
  5. Generate clips, then editUse the approved storyboard as a visual blueprint; arrange shots, voiceover, music and effects in post.
Core strength: visual decisions and shot order are planned before the final clip-generation stage.
Explore this approach ↗
APPROACH / 02

Text → Video Clips

Used for · Adhaiya Ki Bhakti

Turn the approved story into precise scene prompts, attach the approved visual references, generate clips and refine the output.

  1. Master prompt & story approvalProvide the film topic, approve the intro voiceover and review story sections in parts.
  2. Lock the visual referencesApprove the character list, generate character sheets and confirm the locations used by the story.
  3. Prompt individual clipsUse reference images, character-count/position constraints, dialogue, camera and continuity notes.
  4. Inspect and iterateCheck unexpected changes, duplicated characters, actions, framing and other generation issues; revise where needed.
  5. Assemble voice and pictureBring generated clips, narration, music and effects into editing; review continuity and export.
Core strength: test and refine each generated scene while keeping approved story, character and location references close at hand.

AI film creator.
Beginner-first educator.

I started learning AI and Generative AI while pursuing my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication. That curiosity grew into a creative journey—using AI tools to craft cinematic films and explore new ways of storytelling.

AI and GenAI are often surrounded by myths, jargon and confusing claims, which can make the field feel harder for beginners than it really is. I want to break down that confusion and make AI concepts easier to approach, understand and experiment with.

As an AI content creator and educator, I create beginner-friendly content that helps students and newcomers understand AI topics through clear explanations, practical examples and visual storytelling—so they can learn with confidence and use these tools creatively.
